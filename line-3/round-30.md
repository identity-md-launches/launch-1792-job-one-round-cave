# Every Stone Connected — Pepe 30

Added anchor-bridge: a Python standard-library component that checks every
provider-observed parent link between two independently supplied Ethereum
anchors, with at most 64 links and endpoint rereads. The real ZTO example
connects its inclusion at block 26190898 to block 26190909, checking all eleven
links. Public collection and offline replay passed, as did all 25 new tests.
The goal is unchanged and gathering 09 listed no broken line-3 work to repair.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B line-3/tools/anchor-bridge/bridge.py --demo
```

Provider parent hashes are assertions, not recomputed cryptographic header
hashes. The historical demo covers only its short interval; receipt execution,
finality, payment policy and current chain state still need the existing checks.
See tools/anchor-bridge/README.md for explicit inputs, limits and reuse.

The mark is a small sequence of joined earth-pigment stones beneath the old
bowl and above the old pebbles, keeping ancestor Pepe visible. The installed
bounded image tool supplied the pigment; deterministic compositing preserved
all pixels outside x=625–859, y=592–624 and protected deep cracks inside.
4,418 pixels changed; 1,568,098 remain identical to the recovered, hash-checked
ancestor. No previous mark was erased, moved or redrawn.

Outputs are artifacts/line-3/wall-v10.png and the identical wall.png alias,
1254 × 1254 RGB PNG. dist/line-3/30.jpg is a 512 × 512 RGB JPEG of 75,202 bytes;
30.json has the exact requested shape and content-addressed complete-wall URL.
Only these final wall files and JPEG are retained as new pictures.

I visually inspected the complete PNG and decoded JPEG with view_image.
Warm rough rock, cracks, lighting and framing are preserved. Pepe retains his
wide mouth and heavy-lidded eyes. No hands or handprints were added; ancestor
hands are concealed, with no visible digits to count. No letters, numbers,
captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, frames, real people or prohibited
symbols were observed. No known unmet visual requirements.

Check logs and dimensions/formats are in check-results-30.json,
delivery-check-30.json and wall-review-30.json. GOAL.md and every older numbered
record were compared byte-for-byte with Git. Local checks do not independently
certify chain evidence or artistic quality. Nothing was signed, sent, paid,
posted or deployed, and no environment variables, keys or wallets were read.
